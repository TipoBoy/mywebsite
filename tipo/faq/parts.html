Parts prices for Tipo 16V

The part number refers to the FIAT part number. The price shown is the list price for the Genuine FIAT part and does not include any labour charge. Of course some non genuine parts are available and the prices vary but so does the quality. Andy Forsdyke who works at Desirabury kindly supplied most of the information below. If you speak to Andy he will give you a trade discount (please mention where you saw the prices).

Please note that the table is split into two major sections. The left hand columns contain the part numbers and prices for the 5 door Tipo 16V. If the part number or price is different for the 3 door Tipo 16V then the necessary information is displayed in the right hand columns.
