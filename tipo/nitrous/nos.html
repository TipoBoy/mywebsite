Nitrous Oxide

Martin Hodges fitted a nitrous oxide kit to his Tipo 16V in November 2000. Visit his page to read all about it.

Martin used a kit supplied by a company called Highpower Systems. They supply a wide range of kits although you can also pay to have them fitted. Highpower performance products include: fuel and water pumps, nitrous oxide injection systems, progressive nitrous controllers, ignition systems, turbocharging aids and compatabillity products as well as power shifters and boost retard units and water injection systems. This site has a particularly good technical description of how Nitrous Oxide works.

Cicale Racing in Italy fitted Nitrous Oxide to a Tipo 16V for a client.
