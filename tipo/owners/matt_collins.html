Matt Collins

Matts car was registered at Montroe Motors in Chelmsford in January 1993, where he lives, & is a five door in red. He acquired the car in April 1997 from one of his best friends Dad who had owned it from brand new. He had always said to him from the time he first bought the car that as & when he decided to sell it to give him first refusal, which he did when he replaced it with an Alfa 146. The best thing was he only wanted £3,500 for it, which was the price Fiat/Alfa had offered him as a trade in.

The previous owner used the car to commute to & from work every day so after four & a bit years when he bought the car, it had a higher than average 73,000 miles on the clock. The journey travelled was basically motorway or dual carriageway door to door so he thinks maybe that, along with the car always being regularly serviced at a Fiat main dealer has helped contribute to the fact that so far, touch wood, he has not had the slightest bit of trouble. After reading on this web site the trials & tribulations of other owners he considers himself very lucky. The only money he has spent is on things like servicing, handbrake cable, manifold gasket & tyres.

Having now owned the car for nearly four & a half years, it has covered 96,000 miles, he can say it has always been a real pleasure & lots of fun to drive. It is still completely standard apart from what looks like a smaller engined Tipo back section to the exhaust. He had the original wheels refurbished two years ago, they were like brand new when first done but are now beginning to go a bit again but the chap who worked on them did say would deteriorate again in time.
