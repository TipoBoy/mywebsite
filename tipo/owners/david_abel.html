David Abel

David has a 1992 'J' plate Fiat Tipo 16V which has covered 64,000 miles. He loves the car to bits and takes great pride of it. It's a red 5 door 16V with the standard 15 inch wheels. It's got a Pioneer CD stereo, attached to a 300 watt amplifier and 10 speakers (6 on the rear parcel shelf and 2 each in the front doors). There's also a graphic equaliser. The car has also had a new cam belt, after it failed costing him £1700 to fix, otherwise the engine is standard and run on Mobil 1 synthetic motor oil. Yes, oil consumption seems to go up when using better quality oil

David found out that his Tipo actually started life as a Fiat demonstration model and he's the second owner since then.

David says that his Previous car was a Uno Turbo MK1 and he thinks the Tipo is faster and better handling!
