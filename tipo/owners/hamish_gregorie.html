Hamish Gregorie

Hamish was the first person to send me an email after seeing the article in the October 2000 issue of AutoItalia magazine. He has had a 'J' plate 16v for 3 months with some initial bad luck. The timing belt went two weeks after he brought it. He had new valves and two new belts fitted for £750 by an Intergrale garage near Tonbridge Wells.

Hamish was initially after a simple late 1.4 but drove a seidicivalvole and couldn't resist the drilled accelerator pedal.
