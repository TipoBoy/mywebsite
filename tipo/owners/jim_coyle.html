Jim Coyle

Jim loves his white 1995 3 door Tipo 16v. He bought it in October 1998 with 61000 miles on the clock. By September 1999 the car has now done 67000 miles. Jim actually bought the car for his wife Janet as he has a company Astra car but as you can imagine he pinches it whenever he can (especially weekends).

Since buying the car he's had just a couple of problems. The wiper arm started to scratch paint off the nearside wing!! and had to be adjusted. The car also collects stone chips on the rear wheel arches....they seem inevitable. It also needed a new exhaust and flexihose. When he had the belts changed in September 1999 it was discovered that the water pump was worn out and needed replacing which made it a rather expensive visit to the garage.

Jim likes the understated looks of the car, especially good for the "traffic light drag"
