Nareman Virk

Nareman has a J reg 5 door in metallic greenstone. It was originally sold by Edwards of Epsom (now Auto Italia) in March 1992 with ABS, which was good and an alarm, which wasn't. The following year the insurance company insisted on the addition of an immobiliser.

The electric window controller went after about 6 months but was replaced under warranty.

The cat went in 1996 and was replaced with a stainless steel tube.

About this time his mechanic noticed that two of the wheels had been dented on the inner rim by a hamfisted tyre fitter approximately 18 months previously and were replaced by non standard not particularly expensive items.

The clutch slave cylinder went last year and the car now has the dodgy idle problem that many of the contributors to this site seem to have experienced.

It's on its third exhaust - the current one being a nasty Kwik Fit item which is blowing at the back box joint.

The car has a reasonable 49200 on the clock and is in fairly good nick for a 9 year old car.

Narmen has owned six Fiat Tipos since he passed his driving test in 1991 and he strongly believes that the Tipo 16V is the best hot hatch that you can buy. He has been approached by a complete stranger in a car park who offered to buy his car but Nareman says what would he replace it with?

Nareman has not had the best of luck with his two previous Tipo 16V cars. One of those melted it's spark plugs which fell into the engine. Needless to say, his engine was not happy and sounded like a tractor. Luckily, it happened near his house, so he managed to get home. It needed a new head and was in the garage for 2-3 weeks. To make things worse the bottom end of the engine had only been rebuilt about six months earlier and he had been without the car for almost two months. During this rebuild (65,000 miles) he had a new clutch, new brake pads and discs all round and a new radiator.

Then again towards the end of 1999 Nareman spent a fortune on repairing his Tipo. New clutch slave cylinder, new offside CV joint, new cambelt, new cambelt bearing, new cam-cover gasket, new exhaust, new hub-nuts, new gearbox oil, new power steering belt. In the summer of 1999 he had a new gearbox, new brake master cylinder and replaced the sump gasket.

In July 1999 Nareman's gearbox broke, it didn't quite make it to 90,000 miles.
