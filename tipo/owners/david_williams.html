David Williams

David has a 1992 5 door 16 valve Tipo which has has owned and enjoyed since buying it in 1994, following 4 years owning an Uno Turbo. The previous owner on the log book was "Courtney Pine". Its not a particularly popular name so there was a high probability it could be the famous Jazz musician. He was slightly dubious about this when it was mentioned by the dealer but when he checked with Fiat to clarify some of the history they confirmed it. Apparently, he got it as part of the deal for appearing in / supplying music for one of their adverts!

David says it's been a great car to own with a combination of great handling, more than enough poke to suprise people that don't realise the Tipo came in a cooking version and large enough to be useful when you've taken on ambitious house renovations!

The only major problem was the ECU which was replaced under warranty and some minor front end remodelling courtesy of a short sighted female who bottled a right hand turn and turned her car into an immobile obstruction. Oh and some scumbags nicking the CD player using the "Napolitan door peel" technique. Despite these attempts, the car is holding together better than the Turbo which started to get the dreaded tin rot in the doorskins at 4 years old - a testament to the galvanising process introduced to the manufacturing process.

The wheels were changed for TSW blades after he trashed one on a kerb in the snow and found it was only £50 more than the replacement price to replace all 4 and be able to access a better range of tyres. Apart from that the car is pretty standard and has 80k on the clock. David's always used fully synthetic oil which gets a bit expensive as it drinks it on fast motorway use. Other owners he has spoken to have had similar problems with oil consumption but some have said theirs improved beyond 60k miles
