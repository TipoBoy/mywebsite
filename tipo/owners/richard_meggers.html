Richard Meggers

Richard purchased his Sedici back in April 2000. He spotted the car advertised in Autotrader and immediately phoned and bought the car within 24 hours. He paid £2650 for it and it had 62,000 miles with a full Fiat SHE. He used to own a 1990 G reg TIPO 1.4 DGT with a full Zender Body Kit and Factory alloys. When he spotted the sedici he couldn't believe it as he had only ever seen 5 door sedici's, and had nver seen a three door.
