Steve Morrison

Steve has a five door 16V with Recaro seats. He knows quite a lot about the engines as he has had to rebuild it a couple of times. The first time was because the timing belt had snapped during it's life with it's previous owner. They had repaired it but did not set up the balancer shafts correctly. As a result the engine vibrated itself to death. The last I heard from Steve was that he was going to try supercharging his Tipo as a mechanical engineering project. Nobody knows if this ever succeeded. Another alternative was going to be junking the 16V injection engine and fitting a 16V Turbo instead. The engine can be found in Integrales, Themas and he thinks, the early Fiat Coupe Turbo Steve reckons it would cost around £1000 to buy a low mileage one, and another £500 to fit it. This would give an instant 200+ bhp with the potential for a whole lot more. Steve says there are only 5 Tipo 16V cars in the whole of Edinburgh.
