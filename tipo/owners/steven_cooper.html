Steven Cooper

Stevens car was first registered on 24th June 1993, K registration in Manchester by Fiat Motor Company, and driven by Fiat accountant based at Slough. It was fitted with the optional ABS. Steven purchased it on 29th March 1994 from Motor House of Cannock, for the meager price of £7,000 with 15,500 miles on the clock - currently at 57,000 miles with full Fiat service and repaired as required.
