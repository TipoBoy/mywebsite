Danny Organtzis

Danny from Greece is a proud owner of Sedicivalvole. His car is a 1992 model, with ABS, AirCon and all the other regular 16v standards but unfortunately no Recaro interior. It's a cat version. His as removed the airbox and replaced it with a K&N cone filter. He has also fitted an adjustable strut brace, cross-drilled discs and OMP pads. He has some tailor-made shock absorbers to his own specification which are stiffer than standard OE ones but not too stiff like Konis.

Danny's current Tipo is red, but he used to have a second one, a white one, which he sold on to a friend about a year ago. Recently he had to replace the engine due to the carelessness of his father. He borrowed the car from him for a 2000 mile return journey and did not check the oil. It ran out of oil and damaged the bottom end. He finds the oil consumption to be about 1 liter every 1000 kms.

Danny has now fitted a Lancia Thema 16V engine, actualy just the main core of the engine and kept the rest original. I had to do this as I could not find a Tipo engine around.
