Andy Underwood

Andy has had his Sedicivalvole for 3 years. Its a 3 door 93 (L registered) bright red with 79,000 miles. It still looks as good as the day he bought it, although the bumpers have faded slightly. Andy says the car is great.

He recently replaced the radiator as the original one had corroded. A local garage, Les George in Buckhurst Hill, Essex supplied and fitted a new one for £120 including VAT. The cheapest quote he got elsewhere was £130 + VAT.
