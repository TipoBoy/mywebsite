Joaquin Martinez

Joaquin read this web site before buying his Tipo 16V. He lives in Zaragoza and brought the car almost new. It is a 1991 model and only had 45,000 km on it and had been in a garage most of the time. The condition is perfect and it still keeps the original shine in the paint.

Joaquin say that the tipo 16v has a perfect behaviour on road, but in the heavy traffic of Zaragoza it is not easy to drive. The engine doesn't have mych power below 3.000 rpm. So it works irregularily under these conditions.

Joaquin also notices some noise within the vehicle caused by the air getting into the car, probably thought the heating system.
