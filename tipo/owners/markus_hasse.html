Markus Hasse

Markus has a red Tipo SV, with 150 bhp. He is searching for everthing he can use to get more power out of the engine. However he has one proviso, it has to be cheap! Ideally he wants to have up to 200 bhp, and says that he can get 168-174 bhp without doing anything to the head except for new camshafts and a larger throttle body. So far Markus has fitted a Superchip, a "CSC" exhaust system and a cold air feed from by the left hand side fog light.
