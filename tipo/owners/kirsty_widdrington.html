Kirsty Widdrington

Kirsty is a trainee mechanic and works for a company called Rossi Engineering where they work on a whole range of italian cars ranging from Fiat Pandas to Maserati and Ferrari's. Her boss is called Roberto Giordanelli who also works for Auto-Italia magazine as there test driver.

Kirsty used to own a Fiat Tipo 1.4 and had it for 3 years and 3 months then had enough and decided up get the car she'd been dreaming of a 16V.

She bought a metallic blue M reg 3 door priced at £2000. It had a big bore exhaust on it but required some mechanical work which she did not mind. So far she has replaced the wishbones, rear pads, front discs and pads. They have been chaned for red.dot 6 grove discs and medium pads. The cam & tensioner belt have been replaced and it also needs a radiator as half of the fins are missing. Kirsty also needs to change the drop link/antiroll bar bushes.

Kirsty is happy to have managed to save the car in time and is giving it a bit of long over due TLC.

Kirsty says she absolutely loves the car. It's so nice to actually accelerate in 5th gear and get to a ton in what feels like next to nothing.
