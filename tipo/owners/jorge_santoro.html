Jorge Santoro

Jorge owns a Tipo 16V and was very happy until the cam belt broke and all 16 valves were damaged. The whole of the engine head was rebuilt costing a heap of money in Brazil. Jorge is looking for some technical information about the car as he can't find it in Brazil. This is very strange as one of the biggest FIAT factories is located in his home town. FIAT tend not to release too much information about the car and as a result Jorge needs to know if anyone has technical information, electrical diagrams and in particular information about MPFI sensors and actuators.
