Matthew Martin

Matthew has a red 1994 2.0 16 valve 3 door Tipo. It has ABS and about 35,000 miles with no modifications. He says that it is a great car but only about 30 - 40 were sold in New Zealand. They arrived at the end of 1994 and didn't sell that well because they were expensive. In 1995 the importer replaced the interoir with the Recaro seats and trim, and then they sold much better. All of them were 3 door versions, but not all of them had ABS.
