Matt Stafford

Matt has a white 1994 3 door Tipo 16V. Matt had great difficulty obtaining the flexihose section of the exhaust. He found the only way to get this part was to get it from Fiat who ordered it from Italy, but only after he called Fiat UK customer services to complain. The part contains quite a lot of intricate stainless welding and cost a staggering £182.68 including VAT. Now that the Tipo is no longer being produced in the UK he is very concerned that obtaining parts for the car is going to get worse.

In January 2000 Matt had an unfortunate incident with his car on the A1 south bound 5 miles north of Wetherby. The traffic was braking heavily for standing traffic ahead and he was the one at the back. He now definitely knows his Tipo doesn't have ABS!

It wasn't a major impact (about 25mph) but it was enough for the insurance company to right it off. They reckoned on £2400 to fix. the damage is:

  • bonnet
  • drivers side wing
  • drivers side headlight
  • bumper
  • front panel
  • inner and outer grille
  • punctured radiator
  • slightly kinked chassis arm
Matt is looking to rebuild it myself. He's had two independent people look at it and both have said the same thing. The damage to the chassis arm is minor and all forward of the suspension strut. this can be re-jigged for about £60 providing he strips the parts off myself. Then it's a simple bolt on rebuild.

If the insurance company let me have it back then he'll be looking for parts. If not then it would be a great source of stuff for you lot. That will be a shame though 'cos its immaculate (apart from the not ugly front end!) He's only done 58k in it. He'd just put on a new exhaust on and one of those incredibly expensive exhaust flexi hose things (they're £200!).

Matt did finally repair his Tipo. It cost his about £700. He dropped the claim against the insurance company to be able to keep the car. It was quite easily repaired really, the driver's side front chassis arm was slightly bent due to being pulled in by the deformed front panel which kissed a Ford Mondeo tow bar a bit too heavily. He had it jigged out and a new front panel fitted. The rest he did myself as all the panels are just bolted on and he has a mate who is a paint sprayer who did the bonnet, wing and grille. Looks smashing now except for the bumper which still has a split in it.
