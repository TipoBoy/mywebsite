James Hoad

James was reading the web site even before he bought his Tipo. He's a Student at University in Norwich. He bought his silver K reg Sedici back in October 2000, with 67,000 miles on the clock. It's only his second car (after driving a 1.1 Fiesta for 2 years) and he absolutely loves it. The car's completely original except for a large, loud BMC air filter which gives a fantastic growl, and a roar above 3500rpm. He says it's so nice to be finally able to rip up the boy racers in their Novas and XR3s!!

Unfortunately he's had loads of little problems with this car.

James says his car will idle erratically, sometimes cut out, and have lumpy throttle response. It's particularly bad if he has just restarted the car. One time he pulled up at a junction and it idled fine at 850rpm - then just died! It wouldn't start immediately afterwards and he had to push it off the road and wait 5 minutes before it got going again. The problem will only occur when the engine's not fully warmed up. It's fine when cold, and when fully warm, but somewhere inbetween it gives him hassle.

He took it to his local Fiat garage who quoted him £378 to replace some throttle parts including the root of the problem (in their opinion), the idle actuator. As he understands it, the idle actuator cannot be supplied on its own and so he had to order a complete throttle section. Unfortunately the components are on back order and need to be supplied from Italy.

Update from James. The part is now in from Italy and the car's booked in for next week. Someone suggested he try simply cleaning out the idle valve. James spoke to FIAT who reckon they've already tried this. To be fair the car has been slightly better since they looked at it. However, it's by no means fixed and they seem sure it's the actuator so he's going ahead with it.
