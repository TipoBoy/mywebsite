Andy Perkins

Andy and his wife brought their K registered Tipo 16V after visiting this web site in October 1999. Using the Forum they asked a few questions and gathered a few opinions. Eventually they brought the blue one shown below for £2000.

The car has done 99,000 miles but was still in fantastic condition. They put it straight into their local Fiat garage and had the cam belt and bearings changed, as well as a full service costing £470. Since then the trims around the windows have been replaced for it's MOT in December 1999.

Andy's wife uses it mainly to potter around town and hasn't done more than 2000 miles a year, ever. They are both very, very pleased. The car has a full service history, but they cannot account for the Abarth badge and wheels.
