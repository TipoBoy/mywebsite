Angelo Orlando

Angelo has his own Italian web site dedicated to the Tipo 16V. Angelo has remapped the EPROM on his car and it gained 12HP at the top end (160HP), and it now produces 200NM of torque at 5000rpm. There is a lot more HP at the bottom end as well. Angelo has had, what seems a common problem with FIATS, a lot of electrical problems with sensors etc. Angelo is currently trying to get some extra technical information on the Sedicivalvole and also on a 280HP Tipo 16V turbo which he saw in an Italian magazine called "ELABORARE".
