Goran Tomas

When Goran bought the car, the first thing he did was a thorough investigation of everything I've got on the web site and it helped a lot to know all the specific details. Goran tripped over his Sedicivalvole almost by accident. He wasn't looking for a 16V specifically, but rather for Tipo 1.6 because that was in his price range. He looked at the Honda Civic as well. However, he couldn't find anything that looked decent and had a healthy engine with the right price on the windshield. He spent 9 months before he found 16v. Actually, it was a car of a friend of his friend. The owner was going back to Australia (he lives there) and wanted to sell a car ASAP, so he gave him a fair price.

Although the car has a lot of "bumps and bruises" he fell in love the first km he test drove! What great handling, what an engine!! He closed his eyes on the cosmetic details, as well as some other issues (like the car desperately needed new tires, new exhaust system, new engine suspension, etc) and bought it!

Goran is delighted. However he can't yet fully enjoy it because he has to make all these repairs. Since all the money went on the car (and it was a bit over the limit), and he's a student who works on a radio station with rather modest income. Now he's faced with a problem of not being able to get it in the shape he would want it (and that means as good as it can be!)...

Goran hopes that the future will bring a brighter light for him and his Sedicivalvole as it is such an inspiring car. He wants to drive it forever!

The car is a 1991 5 door model and now has 129,000 km. He's still now sure where to take it to be services as he is still asking around for a great (not good) mechanic that can do it right. Some people suggested to him to go to Italy (Trieste) but I'm not sure if that's a good idea, price wise.
