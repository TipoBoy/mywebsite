Richard Portsmouth

Richard has a 1991 J registered black 5 door Tipo. His car doe not have ABS or Recaros, but it has got heated seats and tinted windows. His car has been extensively modified by Barry Waterhouse Engineering and is seriously fast.

The complete impressive specification is as follows:

Engine: Re-bored 84.6mm Mahle Pistons. Re-ground and polished crank with Vandervell shells. Lightened and balanced flywheel. BWE re-mapped eprom. 3 angle valve seats and Thema turbo exhaust valves.

Suspension: Standard Tipo dampers on front and Fiat Coupe on rear with Eibach progressive springs all round. OMP front strut brace.

Exhaust: Remus full system. Stainless steel cat replacement pipe with resonator.

Induction: Freeflow JR panel filter.
