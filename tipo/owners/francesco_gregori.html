Francesco Gregori

Francesco has a weakness for Italian cars. His Sedicivalvole is a black, 5-door which was first registered in 1992 (on a J reg). In his view this is the more desirable is the two versions having 148bhp and being easier MOT etc. The mileage has literally just reached 90,000 miles, of which he is responsible for only the last 2-3,000.

There have been two previous owners, with the last one (Emma) being a particularly fastidious one. She had it for some six years and looked after it very well (which was the reason he bought it actually), ensuring all the mechanicals were cared for irrespective of cost. It is a very original car, with refurbished alloys and numerous new parts. The body work is good for its age but it still has a couple of parking "dinks" from its first owner (marked on the AA report that Emma had performed before she bought it). The interior is as new, and all the electrics work surprisingly well. The service history is absolutely superb, with a file inches thick. It simply flew through its MOT last November. He adores the car and find it is very addictive, especially the induction roar.
