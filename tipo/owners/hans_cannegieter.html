Hans Cannegieter

Hans has a black December 1992 Tipo 16v with Abarth skirts and bumpers. It has covered 75,000 kms and he's very satisfied with it. This is his second 16v Tipo, it consumes 1 liter of fuel every 10 kilometers and 1 liter of oil every 2000 kilometers. He intends to mount a turbocharged Thema engine when this one breaks down, but hopes that will not be for at least 2 to 3 years. Hans is 187cm tall and says that the only thing wrong with the car is it's lack of head room. This is caused by the seats being too high and roof too low when fitted with an electric sunroof.
