Cleiton Krause

Cleiton has a 16V Tipo 3 door with 157,000 km and it's worth about US$7000 but still feels like new. He has got it up to 210km/h and thinks it is possible to go faster. The car is standard with a standard exhaust system. The two biggest problems he's had were with the cam belt jumping because the tension was lost and the transmission.

Cleiton loves his car and wants to install a K&N air filter to it. When the exhaust fails he also wants to fit a straight through system. He is also thinging of a replacement ECU chip since he can get them for US$200.

Cleiton says that fortunately the parts that seem to break can usually be salvaged off the FIAT Tempra which is still made in Brazil. These parts include valves, belts, disc brakes and oil filters.

It turns out that in the extreme south of Brazil, where Cleiton lives, the gasoline does not have 22% alcohol but contains MTBE instead. Cleiton uses VW spark plugs with 3 eletrodes, because they are half the price of the original FIAT items. He has also tried Bosh Super 4 but did not notice any difference in performance between the 3 types. To help the car get as much cold fresh air as possible he has moulded a small piece of plastic and placed it infront of the air duct. This seemed to give a bit more power and was free.

During the cars life it's used 12 tyres, 1 disc brake from a Tempra, 4 brake pads (2 from Tipo and 2 from Tempra, of which the Tipo pads where better), 2 ignition leads, 1 halogen lamp, and 1 distributor.
