Andy Watt

Andy is the recent owner of a Tipo 16v and this site has become his bible for everything Tipo. Andy was fortunate last June to pick up an immaculate totally standard prime example, it had full documented history and only 38,000 on the clock. One year on and he has resisted modifying it in any way and has clocked up only 2,800 miles. It has just sailed through the mot and having once scoffed at anything with Fiat on the back he can truly say he is a reformed owner.
