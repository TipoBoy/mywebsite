Trevor Byne

Trevor has a white 1992 5-door 16V with ABS, a BBR chip producing about 166 bhp and 70,000 miles. He bought it in November 1998. The bodywork is still excellant although the central locking is a bit erratic. The car also has an irritating water leak if parked facing downhill. Performance is great but at a price of only achieving 25 mpg.
