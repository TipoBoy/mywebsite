Steve Aston

Steve bought a 1992 16v Tipo in lovely condition, but the previous owner had messed around with it quite a bit. The gear lever was shortened so it had a terrible gear change. Steve put synthetic oil in the gearbox which helped a lot, but his main problem appears to be lack of power (relative). He says it revs very smoothly and uses no oil. It has a K&N conversion filter kit which he is not sure about as it didn't work on his BMW and had to revert back to the panel filter.
