Dennis Rossi

Dennis's first Tipo, a green example could do 235Km/h. He had this car for two years and thought it was great. It had 195x50R15 tyres but he has seen Tipos with 16" wheels. He claims the Tipo wheel arches are quite accommodating and wider tyres should not be a problem.

The car was taken to the biggest FIAT dealer in South America to have the timing belt replaced. After 3 days the garage phoned to to give him the bad news. It appeared that they had lifted the car on an elevator attaching the arms of the elvator to just the side skirts of the car. As a mechanic was working on the engine the cars side skirts gave way causing Dennis's car to be dropped onto the floor of the garage. Fortunately the mechanic escaped in time but the car was written off. The garage gave Dennis another Tipo, this one was grey and in better condition.

Dennis has since taken his grey Tipo to have the belt replaced. After they were changed the car was never the same. The valves were really noisy and after 12km, it broke down. So, again he had to take the car (this time to a FIAT dealer), and they did just an excellent job! The engine is like new, the power has increased considerably.

Dennis has made some modifications to his grey Tipo. It now has 16" wheels with 205/45 tyres. It has a JK exhaust with a sporty back muffler (which sounds just great). The springs need replacing and Dennis wants to lower it a bit.
