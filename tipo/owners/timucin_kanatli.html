Timuçin Kanatli

Timuçin has an Italian 3 door 1994 model Tipo 2.0 ie 16V. He recently added multi-throttle bodies and got more power than he expected. Went up from 152 bhp 210 Nm to 171 bhp and 235 Nm.

As you can see from the pictures it's not possible to fit the throttle bodies under the standard bonnet so Timuçin has fitted something akin to the Subaru Impreza Turbo bonnet scoop. This is giving a ram air effect as well, but he has to take care in hard rain.

Another interesting points: When he put the isolated manifold on, the manifold pressure dropped 50% on idle. The ecu started to correspondingly only use 50% of the injection and advance maps. So he decided to remove the map sensor. He put a simple circuit connected to the (new linear) throttle position sensor, the output is connected to the map input of ecu. Now he is simulating map values as a function which is nearly the opposite of the tipo's. This gives a very fast throttle response which is what he was dreaming of.

He then found where the lambda (O2) sensor is handled in the program and removed that too. Now at any rpm and any load he can get the exact CO% level he wants. The program reads injection duration and just uses it. This is no longer effected by the the lambda's value. He finds the best power is between 3.5% and 5.5% CO.
