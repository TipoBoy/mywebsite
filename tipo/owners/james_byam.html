James Byam

James' car is a 1992 (J registered) 5 door. When James brought the car in September 19992 it had been used as a demonstrator for 9 months clocking up 12,000 miles.

James relocated in 1998 from the UK to Trinidad & Tobago in the West Indies. He took the car with him and it's now covered some 99,000 miles.

James has has no real problems with the car, only those attributed to normal wear and tear. However, sourcing parts in extremely difficult. For example the master brake cylinder/servo unit failed recently and James was without the car for 2 1/2 months waitng for a replacement. None of the UK parts dealers his contacts could find seemed to know what they were doing and he ended up getting the unit from Italy via a dealer in Florida!!

Like most of us, James thinks it's a brute of a car which he believes has been underrated by most.
