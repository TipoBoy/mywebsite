Tony Ghirardi

Tony has only had his Sedicivalvole since April and he says it was like a breath of fresh air to own an Italian car. He would now never dream of getting rid of it. Having previously owned a Golf GTi 16v he says it was a slight shock getting into the Tipo as the Golf tended to be foot down and steer (Rather boring) whereas the Tipo needs work to get the best performance....and boy do you get it!!

Tony is now looking to get the car performance tuned. he is also a member if the FIAT club which sounds like a good idea. This is split into different sections for each part of the country. The London branch has had a number of meetings, the latest of which was a stand at the London Classic Car Show. During the largest ever Italian Car Day at Brooklands he counted at least 5 Sedici's all in superb condition. Next year it is going to be a FIAT special so looks like a "must attend".
