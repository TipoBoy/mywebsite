Jo Badger

Jo Badger drives a lowered silver 5 door 1991 Fiat Tipo 2.0 16V. The car has had some problems with excessive (<20 mpg) fuel consumption and very noticeable steps in the power band. These have mostly been fixed with new distributor cap, rotor arm and new leads. Unfortunately she finds driving it too much like hard work. Her husband however has had some mental cars in the past and has plans for future modifications. Think this could be one to look out for.
