Cildemac Marques

Cildemac has a 1995 16V Tipo and is unhappy with the changes FIAT make to the cars for the Brazillian market.

Cildemac was feeling very disapointed for living in Brazil and suffering all the problems they have with traffic. He decided to replace the ECU in his car for $150 USD and it made a huge improvement to his car. Using a small electronic timer he was able to improve his times as below:

1.2 seconds faster to get to 100 km/h
1.7 seconds faster to cover the first 400m.

He wasn't that impressed so he had the chip tuned some more and was very pleased with the end result.

2.0 seconds faster to get to 100 km/h
2.6 seconds faster to cover the first 400m.

Cildemac finds it difficult to improve his car as he can't find many parts in Brazil. If he wanted to import the goods from North America then he would have to pay 100% duty.
