Details of Brooklands Meeting

Food

Important things first! Don't forget to bring something to eat and drink as I don't think there will be any stalls to buy food from.

Entry Fee

If you pre-book by phoning 01932 857381 the entry fee is £6 for adults, £4 for children and £5 for OAP. Families are £14 for 2 adults and 2 children.

Stickers

Don't forget to place your car number in the rear windows of the car. If you have not got one then let me know and I will try and get one to you before the event. They serve several purposes:
  • It's a bit of fun, although I'm not sure of the legality of having a number displayed on the side of your car.
  • It's some free advertising for the web site
  • You will be able to know who's car is who's. In the future, I did not have time for this event, I will try and get some name/number badges made up.

Meeting Points

The event starts at 10am and finishes at 4pm. Due to the short opening hours and the fact that it gets dark early I suggest we try and get there as early as possible. However I'm sure nobody want's to get up early on a Sunday so the times below are based on arriving at 11:30am.

On previous occations we've met at various designated meeting points before moving onto Brooklands. At each meeting point we have waited for everyone whoose supposed to be meeting there before moving on to the next meeting point.

Unfortunately this meeting is only a week away so it won't be possible to get a definitive list of people who will be meeting at each point before the event. Therefore I'm proposing that once the designated time is reached (see the times below) then those already at the meeting point leave for the next. Therefore it's imperative you get to the meeting point before the designated time. If you miss the time then head for the next meeting point. Failing that we'll see you there!

The distance from Meeting Point A to meeting point B is about 40 miles. This should take about 50 minutes. Therefore those meeting at Point A should be there by 9:35am. Leave meeting Point A at 9:35am to arrive at Meeting Point B at 10:25am.

From meeting Point C around the M25 to meeting point B is probably 1 hour. Those meeting at Point C should be there by 9:25am. Leave meeting point C at 9:25am to arrive at meeting point B at 10:25am.

The distance from Meeting Point B to meeting point D is about 20 miles but that's around the busiest part of M25. This should take about 45 minutes. Therefore those meeting at Point B should be there by 10:25am. Leave meeting Point B at 10:25am to arrive at Meeting Point D at 11:10am.

From Meeting Point D to Brooklands is just a short 20 minute drive down the A3. Last time we followed a road parallel to the A3 and it was very difficult to maintain contact with one another. We have now worked out how we can get back onto the A3 and the journey should now be straight forward. If we leave Meeting Point D and 11:10am we should arrive at Brooklands at 11:30am.
