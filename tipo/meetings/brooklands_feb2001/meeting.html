Brooklands February 18th 2001

Not a particularly well attended event. My car was still off the road so went in the Escort. Met up at the event with Alexis Grant, Keith Meredith and Martin Hodges.

We talked about cars but there wasn't very much in the form of entertainment put on by the organisers. About the only thing to watch was some passenger rides in a rally prepared Mitsubishi Evolution and Vauxhall Astra.
