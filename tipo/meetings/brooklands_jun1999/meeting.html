Brooklands June 25th 1999

The was the first meeting organised by myself through the website for owners of the Fiat Tipo 16V. It went surprisingly well which made all the hard work organising meeting points worth while. On route we met up with other owners at various points and by the time we got to Brooklands there were five Tipo 16V in the convoy. It was great to at last be able to put faces to the people using the forum. Here are a few pictures of the event.
