Ickworth Park 18th July 1999

The second meeting of the club was held at Ickworth Park, near Bury St Edmunds, East Anglia. Various meeting points were set up en-route to enable various convoys of Tipos to form before entering the Stately Home en-mass. A couple of owners met us there but we had a grand total of 11 Tipo 16V cars which was quite a site. So much so that Auto Italia magazine took a few photos of the cars, unfortunately I've been unable to get hold of them. The cars that turned up belonged to:
  • John Lee
  • Mike Jukes
  • Roy Tomlinson
  • Nigel Gladstone
  • Andy Forsdyke
  • Alex Wakefield
  • Alexis Grant
  • George Roberts
  • Ben Sleeman
  • John Hart
  • Nareman Virk

Nareman's car even won "Best of show".
