Details of Brooklands Meeting

AUTO ITALIA MAGAZINE

PRESS RELEASE 09/08/00 to Fiat clubs

Autumn Italian Car Day - Brooklands Sunday October 8th

The Autumn Italian Car Day will be held at Brooklands on Sunday October 8th This is another opportunity this year for Italian car owners and admirers to enjoy the unique atmosphere of historic Brooklands.

This informal one day meeting will be held in the main Brooklands Museum area and will feature club and trade displays. There will be runs during the day for the smaller and older cars and motorcycles to ascend the infamous test hill. The Corse Giordanelli on the airfield will not be open on this occasion.

Auto Italia magazine would like to take this late season opportunity to photograph owners classic cars before they are stored away for the winter months. There are some features that we would like to revisit so if you have one of the following please come along. All 1950s and pre-war Fiat models. Fiat X1/9. Fiat 130 Coupe and Saloon; Fiat 850 Coupe, Spider and Saloon. Fiat 500 (all models, but unmodified); Fiat 131, 132 and Argenta; Fiat Croma. Fiat 126; Fiat 127 and any Autobianchi.

Anyone with a classic in good original condition or one that has been restored is invited to attend the special photo sessions that will be set up at various locations on the Brooklands site.

Brooklands is in Weybridge, Surrey, close to the A3 and M25 junction. Entrance is £7 per person (£6 OAP, £5 children) payable on the day at the main Brooklands Road entrance. For further details contact 01462 678205.
