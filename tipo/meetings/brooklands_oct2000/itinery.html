Important things first! Don't forget to bring something to eat and drink as I don't think there will be any stalls to buy food from.

Entry Fee

The entry fee to Brooklands is £7 for adults, £5 for children and £6 for OAP.

Stickers

Don't forget to place your car number in the rear windows of the car. They serve several purposes:
  • It's a bit of fun, although I'm not sure of the legality of having a number displayed on the side of your car.
  • It's some free advertising for the web site
  • You will be able to know who's car is who's. In the future, I did not have time, I will try and get some name/number badges made up.

Times

The event starts at 10am. However we have several owners travelling a large distance and that would mean too early a start for them. I'm therefore aiming to arrive at Midday. Dispite all the planning we are usually late. I don't really want to push the times forward 30 minutes to allow for this so instead please try your best to reach your designated meeting points on time.

The distance from Meeting Point A to meeting point B is about 40 miles. This should take about 50 minutes. Therefore those meeting at Point A should be there by 10:05am. Leave meeting Point A at 10:05am to arrive at Meeting Point B at 10:55am.

From meeting Point C around the M25 to meeting point B is probably 1 hour. Those meeting at Point C should be there by 9:55am. Leave meeting point C at 9:55am to arrive at meeting point B at 10:55am.

The distance from Meeting Point B to meeting point D is about 20 miles but that's around the busiest part of M25. This should take about 45 minutes. Therefore those meeting at Point B should be there by 10:55am. Leave meeting Point B at 10:55am to arrive at Meeting Point D at 11:40am.

From Meeting Point D to Brooklands is just a short 20 minute drive down the A3. Last time we followed a road parallel to the A3 and it was very difficult to maintain contact with one another. We have now worked out how we can get back onto the A3 and the journey should now be straight forward. If we leave Meeting Point D and 11:40am we should arrive at Brooklands at 12pm.

Nearly all of us are contactable via mobil phones so it's easy to notify one another of delays etc. Therefore don't get stressed if your running late. All I ask is that you let the people you're meeting know. After all, we want the the journey there to be part of the event.
