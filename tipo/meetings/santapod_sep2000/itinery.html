Details of Santa Pod Meeting

The Event

Santa Pod's biggest drag race of the year. Internationally televised European finals. Sensational drag racing action from over 200 of the finest race teams in Europe plus:
  • FIM Drag Bikes
  • FIA Top Fuel Dragsters
  • FIA Pro Stock
  • FIA Top Methanol Dragsters and Funny Cars
  • Over 20 supporting classses of cars and bikes
  • Thrilling Friday night qualifying session from 4pm (subject to track/weather conditions)
  • Spectacular Jet Car Demonstrations

Entry Fee

Children under 16 go free if accompanied by an adult. Saturday is £16 in advance, £20 on the gate. Sunday is also £16 in advance, £20 on the gate. A weekend ticket for both Saturday and Sunday is £30 in advance or £35 on the gate. To book tickets phone +44 (0) 8700 782828. Advance tickets will be on sale until Tuesday 5th September.
