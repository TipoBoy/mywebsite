Future Meetings

Stanford Hall Auto Italia Consours - Sunday 26th May 2002

Featuring the Auto Italia Concours, Maserati Club Concours, Ferrari Owners' Club display of exotica and displays by other Italian car clubs, this promises to be an opportunity to see some excellent concours quality Italian cars. Other attractions include Auto Italia's Forumla 1 Benetton, trade stands, an auto jumble and auto test, family orientated activities, refreshments and an entrance concession to the stately home.

Brooklands Summer Italian Car Festival - Sunday 28th July 2002

For a historic flavour visit Brooklands this summer. See the hill climb for pre-1940 Italian cars and motorcycles, and demonstrations or historic cars. There will be Italian car club displays, a Concours d'Elegance and an auto test, along with a variety of family activities including quad bikes, and a trade village. Also see Brooklands' refurbished clubhouse and museum, aviation museum, restored banked track and Grand Prix Colection.

Brooklands Autoum Italian Car Festival - October 6th 2002

A late season opportunity for owners to display their classic Italian cars before the winter arrives. Selected cars will have the opportunity to run-up the test hill
