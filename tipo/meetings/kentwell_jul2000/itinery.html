Food

Important things first! Don't forget to bring something to eat and drink. From what I can gather the event is basically a large picnic in the grounds of a stately home. Therfore feel free to bring a picnic hamper and blanket. Lets hope the weather holds out.

Entry Fee

The entry fee to Kentwell Hall is £5.50 for adults, £3.30 for children and £4.75 for OAP. This allows you full access to the grounds and Hall. There are cheaper prices listed on the web site if you only want access to the grounds but I'm not sure whether they apply this Sunday.

Route

Please familiarise yourself with the meeting points. Meeting point C is that last place we will meet before we head up the M11 towards Kentwell Hall. Kentwell Hall is located just a couple of miles North of Sudbury in Suffolk. I've looked at a road map and also contacted the AA and the best route is to go North up the M11 until Junction 8 (Bishop's Stortford). Turn East onto the A120 to Braintree. Turn onto the A131 and head North to Sudbury and then North on the A134 to Kentwell Hall. Please make a note of these directions just in case we get split up en route.

Stickers

Don't forget to place your car number in the rear windows of the car. They serve several purposes:
  • It's a bit of fun, although I'm not sure of the legality of having a number displayed on the side of your car.
  • It's some free advertising for the web site
  • You will be able to know who's car is who's. In the future, I did not have time, I will try and get some name/number badges made up.

Times

The event starts at Midday and finishes about 4 or 5 pm. We should really aim to get there at Midday to make the most of it.

The distance from Meeting Point A to meeting point B is about 40 miles. This should take about 50 minutes. Therefore those meeting at Point A should be there by 9:10. Leave meeting Point A at 9:10am to arrive at Meeting Point B at 10am.

The distance from Meeting Point D to meeting point B is about 20 miles but that's either across the outskirts of London or around the busiest part of M25. This should take about 45 minutes. Therefore those meeting at Point D should be there by 9:15. Leave meeting Point D at 9:15am to arrive at Meeting Point B at 10am.

From meeting Point B around the M25 to meeting point C is probably 1 hour. Those meeting at Point B should be there by 10am. Leave meeting point B at 10am to arrive at meeting point C at 11am.

The distance from Meeting Point C to Kentwell Hall is about 66 miles. It should take us just over an hour (it's all dual carridgeway or motorway). Those meeting at Point C should therefore be there by 11am. If we leave Meeting Point C at 11am we should arrive at Kentwell Hall at 12pm.

Nearly all of us are contactable via mobil phones so it's easy to notify one another of delays etc. Therefore don't get stressed if your running late. All I ask is that you let the people you're meeting know. After all, we want the the journey there to be part of the event.
