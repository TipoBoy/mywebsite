John Whalley Ltd

Dear Sir,

Thank you for your letter of the 3rd February regarding your 2 ltr Turbo Fiat Tipo project.

I regret that having discussed your project with our technicians and having given the project due consideration, non of the technicians wish to be team leader thereon. Without enthusiasm for a project, it is not a good idea for us to start out on it.

Although we have developed similar installations for my own rally cars and know many of the problems that can occur, we would not be prepared to embark on a development project such as you propose on a fixed budget. There are a number of uncharted areas that such a project could become entangled, the firm of which is the aerodynamics of the vehicle itself. For example without knowledge of the pressure zones in front of and within the engine bay the inter cooler position could be critical to the whole enterprise, although it may work at one speed, pressure can change and cooling ceases at a higher speed, you could be lucky with the first guess or it may need much research. (Nissan's rally car failed some years ago because the car was unsuitable for inter cooler positioning, without re-styling the car they could not make it work). Thus the costing of such a project would be nigh to impossible to forecast.

If you wish to build such a car, you must take responsibility for its success or failure yourself, as you are the chief designer, it would be difficult to find a Company willing to offer you a Warranty on such a project with regard to its successful outcome, never mind let you try it before you decide to pay for it.

We do not rule out that it may all end happily but it would be more by luck than judgement if it is to be run on a fixed budget.

Thus we regret to confirm that we do not wish to become involved with this project.

Yours sincerely

John Whalley
