All of the 16v Integrale engines had Garret T3 turbos fitted but with different specifications for the cat cars which have smaller turbine and compressor wheels. It follows then that the earlier cars had turbos that will flow more air and thus produce more power but at the expense of some extra lag.

The cost of a reconditioned unit (either type) is £350.00 + VAT on an exchange basis, but if you wanted to change an Evo cat turbo for the earlier type or vice versa then there would be a £100 surcharge. New turbos are approx. £750 but a reconditioned one is as good as new.
