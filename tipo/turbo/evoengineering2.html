The later cars were nominally more powerful but I think this was a marketing exercise - they certainly feel slower and I suspect that the real maximum power was about 195 BHP. The engine management system on the later cars is quite different and the maximum boost allowable by this is slightly higher than with the earlier cars - there are no internal changes in the engine to enable more power.

250 BHP with the smaller turbo is certainly possible but it will be working harder which will have some effect on its life.

The big problem with one off conversions is that they always take much longer to do than even the wildest estimate - there is simply no way to be sure of how long the job will take. We inevitably end up charging for many less hours than we have spent and the job becomes uneconomic.
