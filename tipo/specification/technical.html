Technical Specification of my Tipo 16V



ENGINE
2023cc, 4 cylinder, 16 valve Weber IAW electronic fuel injection. 84.6mm bore, 90mm stroke. Compression ratio 11.6:1. Forged aluminum, custom designed Venonlia pistons. Steel billet "T" cross section con rods, balanced end over end. Fully balanced crank. Inlet valves 34.5mm exhaust valves 28.5mm. Triple interferance valve springs. Counter rotating balancer shafts locked and its double sided toothed belt removed. K&N 57i induction kit. NGK B9EGV spark plugs . Gas flowed cylinder head and inlet manifold. Lancia Thema Turbo head gasket. Cosworth polished alloy oil separator tank. Cosworth BD3 camshaft profile. Three angle valve seats. Rising rate adjustable fuel pressure regulator. Jaguar XJ6 throttle body. Modified Superchip and fully mappable Unichip piggyback ECU. Rev limit set to 9000 rpm. Cam cover and inlet manifold power coated in red. Custom made 2.5" exhaust system with single rear box and 3" tailpipe. Click here for complete history of rebuild.

TRANSMISSION
Standard FIAT 5 speed manual gearbox. Standard flywheel, fully lightened and balanced. Group N 4-paddle clutch.

SUSPENSION
Spax uprated springs, standard FIAT dampers; lowered 35mm all round.

BRAKES
Front - 285mm Tarox 12 groove discs with Tarox pads. Rear - Standard FIAT discs and pads.

WHEELS & TYRES
7×16in TSW Evo R 6 spoke wheels with 205/45R16 Yokohama A520 rubber.

BODYWORK
FIAT Metallic Ming Red paint (code 144). Debadged. Plain (non Sedicivalvole) rear tailgate handle. Smoked oval side repeaters.

INTERIOR
Momo Race Airleather Aluminum Gear Knob.
Momo Race Steering Wheel.

ICE
Pioneer KEH-P8200RDS head unit with Multi-CD/DSP control, FM/MW/LW Tuner and Dolby B&C tape deck. 4x35W power output. Pioneer CDX-P610 6 disk Multi-CD player in boot. Infinity Reference Series 4612cf 2 way 4×6" custom fit front speakers. 4×4" Kenwood KFC-HQ102 2 way custom fit rear speakers.

SECURITY
Lots of it, thank-you.

PERFORMANCE
Max power 181 bhp @ 6375 rpm. Max torque 164 lbft @ 5187 rpm. Max speed 48 mph (1st gear), 75 mph (2nd gear), 110 mph (3rd gear), 145 mph (4th gear). Theoretical maximum speed in 5th gear 187 mph. Click here for a complete power plot.
