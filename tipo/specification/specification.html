Details of Engine Rebuild

The piston rings in my engine recently burnt out because the engine had been running rich. The engine would therefore need to be stripped down and rebuilt. This presented itself as an ideal opportunity to build it to a higher specification.

There is only one garage in the UK that has experience of building a FIAT Tipo 16V to racing specification. With a modified cylinder head as well as several other minor modifications including the exhaust, the cooling system and air intake this race version produced 179 bhp.

Andy Giles, the cars owner, unfortunately crashed this Tipo in his last race ending up in a gravel trap. On examination of the engine it appeared that gravel had got under the cam belt causing it to jump a few teeth resulting in a set of bent valves. Both Andy's engine and my engine were now in need of a rebuild. After several discussions with the garage, we decided that both engines should be rebuilt to the following specification:
  • Forged Aluminum Custom Designed Pistons made by Venolia in USA
  • 11.6:1 compression ratio.
  • Steel billet "T" cross section con rods allowing engine to exceed 9,000 rpm.
  • Lightened flywheel.
  • Fully balanced bottom end.
  • Group N paddle clutch to withstand the higher power output.
  • Gas flowed and ported cylinder head, valves and inlet manifold.
  • Triple interference valve springs.
  • Camshaft to custom profile based on Cosworth BDA profile.
  • Dastek's Unichip - a fully mappable piggyback ECU.
I further opted for the following items to allow the engine to breath better and also to supply the enough fuel that would be required.
  • Throttle body off a Jaguar XJ6 to allow more air into engine.
  • Adjustable fuel pressure regulator to provide extra fuel to injectors.
  • Custom built 2 1/2" straight through exhaust system with a single rear silencer.
As you can imagine this is not going to be cheap. The pistons and con rods alone are roughly £1200. The total cost is estimated at about £4000. For this we should get about 220 - 230 bhp out of the engine whilst it should still remain tractable for road use.
