Introduction

I'm glad you could make it!. You've just found the worlds best site dedicated to the Fiat Tipo 16V. Don't just take my word for it though have a look around. You will find loads of, hopefully interesting, stuff. To get you started, have a look at the "Articles" page for reviews of the Tipo, the "FAQ" page for a wealth of useful information and the "Meetings" page for stuff we get up to. To talk to other 16V owners venture into the "Forum".

The Fiat Tipo 16V was my passion from 1994 when I bought a 6 month old example for £8600 with 14,000 miles on the clock. In 2005 I moved to Canada and sold the Tipo with over 100,000 miles on the clock to Torque Italia. Hopefully it still lives on somewhere. During my ownership I loved the car and spent whatever was necessary to keep it on the road including three engines.

I took the Fiat Coupe to Canada with me. I lived in Calgary and enjoyed driving it around the Rocky Mountains for 5 years. In January 2011 I moved to Houston in Texas. I had to put it into storage in Canada because it can't be imported into the USA. In November 2023 the Fiat was over 25 years old. This meant it no longer had to meet the current DOT or EPA standards. I therefore imported it into the USA.
