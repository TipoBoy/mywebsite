30% Brighter Main Beam

Halfords part number 448B.

These use Xenon gas technology to give up to 30% brighter light using the same wattage (55W). They are a direct replacement H1 style bulb. I replaced them because one original bulb had blown and I thought I might as well try these bulbs. Unfortunately I can't compare these with with orginal bulbs as I never drove the car using the original bulbs.

They are road legal and 'E' marked.
