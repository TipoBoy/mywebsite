PACE Updated Alloy Radiator

These radiators made specifically for a group of Coupe owners by PACE are 100% aluminium and are 45% more efficent at cooling than the original unit. Size of core is 40×360×530mm. The radiator is also lighter than the original and takes about an extra litre of coolant. The radiator will probably also last easily 10-15 years. The radiators are hand made and take about 6 hours to make.

The car now has much cooler running temperatures and slower temperature soak when sat in traffic. The radiator also cools the temperature back down very quickly once the car is moving.

Make sure that you only use one of the top two fixing bolts. The front of the car flexes and the stiff aluminium end caps can crack where as the original plastic end caps of the FIAT radiator will flex a little.
