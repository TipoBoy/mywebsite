2.4 Engine Conversion (2007)

19th March (1 Hour)

  • Removed electric connections to radiator temperature sensor. Will need new ones as old ones have melted.
  • Removed undertray. Will need new bolts for rear of undertray and plug in wishbones.
  • Loosened bolts holding each half of the CV joints together.
  • Removed battery and battery tray.

22nd March (2 Hours)

  • Tried to remove wishbones but the subframe needs lowering first.
  • Removed chargecooler.
  • Removed radiator support bracket, radiator and coolant hoses.
  • Removed air intake from turbo.

25th March (1.5 Hours)

  • Started to remove downpipe.
  • Removed camshaft cover, coil packs and fuel injector leads.
  • Removed coolant overflow tank.
  • Removed hoses between turbo and intercooler.
  • Fitted bags over CV joints to protect them from dust.

26th March (0.5 Hours)

  • Removed wide and narrow band lamda probes.
  • Finished removing downpipe.

27th March (1.5 Hours)

  • Removed oil cooler hoses.
  • Removed alternator.
  • Removed exhaust manifold.
  • Removed turbo.
  • Removed metal water duct from behind exhaust manifold.

28th March (1 Hour)

  • Removed power steering hoses.

31st March (2 Hours)

  • Removed strut brace.
  • Removed fuel lines from fuel rail.
  • Removed all electrical connections to rear of engine.
  • Removed all air pressure hoses from plenum chamber.
  • Started to remove gear linkages.
  • Removed water hoses from thermostat.

1st April (3.5 Hours)

  • Removed remaining hoses from rear of engine block.
  • Finished removing gear cable linkage clips.
  • Removed front bumper, front mount intercooler and side lights.
  • Raised car so front is 90cm above the ground.
  • Removed thermostat.
  • Fitted sling around engine and connected to crane.
  • Loosened front, gearbox and rear engine mounts.
  • Loosened front anti-roll bar, powersteering rack, wishbones and lowered front subframe.
  • Lowered engine to floor using a crane.

2nd April (2 Hours)

  • Removed driveshaft between differential and right hand bracket.
  • Removed bolts holding gearbox to block but it wouldn't come off.

3rd April (1 Hour)

  • Removed clutch slave cylinder and gearbox pulled off easily.
  • Removed clutch cover plate.
  • Removed clutch.
  • Removed flywheel.
  • Removed metal sheet between gearbox and engine block.

26th April (1.5 Hours)

  • Removed all connections to the air intake and removed it.
  • Removed camshaft cover.

28th April (2.5 Hours)

  • Removed plastic ends off head which are part of the cambelt cover.
  • Removed head bolts in diagonally opposite order from outside to inside.
  • Removed sump plug and drained oil.
  • Turned engine upside down and removed oil breather pipe off sump.
  • Removed bracket holding driveshaft to block.
  • Removed dipstick.

29th April (2.5 Hours)

  • Removed Exhaust shield mounting bolt from head.
  • Removed water temperature thermostat from head.
  • Made wooden box to ship head to Barbz.

30th April (0.5 Hours)

  • Removed camshafts from head.

1st May (3 Hours)

  • Removed water and oil hoses of Turbo.
  • Packed Turbo, exhaust manifold and head in boxes ready for posting.

2nd May

  • Posted Turbo, exhaust manifold and head to Barbz.

3rd May (1 Hour)

  • Removed driveshaft support from rear of engine block.
  • Managed to lever off the oil sump.
  • Removed oil pickup.
  • Removed 6 bolts holding on the crank pully. Must remember to line up the white dot on the pully with that on the shaft.
  • Removed 3 bolts holding on the oil breather. Discovered that the rubber O ring was ineffective causing oil to leak down the block.
  • Removed knock sensors.
  • Removed crank position sensor and the metal bracket carrying their wires to the plugs.
  • Removed hose joining thermostat with rear of block.
  • Removed oil temp and pressure sensor that fit onto the block using a Y connector. Needs a new washer as old one was oval. Use thread lock gel when refitting.
  • Removed metal 90 degree water tube connection to rear of block. Needs a new washer.

5th May (3.5 Hours)

  • Removed power steering pulley and pump.
  • Removed power steering reservoir to give access to paint body.
  • Removed front engine mount.
  • Removed slam panel.
  • Undid plastic cable shield.
  • Removed towing eye.
  • Removed bumper mounting points.
  • Removed clutch pipe bracket.

6th May (5.5 Hours)

  • Removed all rust from engine bay and bolt on parts.
  • Removed wheel arch liners.
  • Masked engine bay ready for painting.

7th May (1 Hour)

  • Painting.

8th May (1.5 Hours)

  • Dismantled power steering reservoir bracket ready for painting.
  • Painting.

9th May (2.5 Hours)

  • Cleaned front engine mount.
  • Drilled out sheared bolt from battery tray.
  • Removed wire clip off battery tray.
  • Dismantled fan housing.
  • Painting.

10th May (2 Hours)

  • Prepared engine mount for painting.
  • Dismantled bonnet catch ready for painting.
  • Completely dismantled throttle body, injectors ready for cleaning and polishing.
  • Had difficulty removing rubber grommets from inlet manifold.
  • Painting.

22nd May (3 Hours)

  • Started to tidy up wiring loom down LHS and RHS of engine bay.
  • Cleaned fan and fitted back onto housing.

28th May (3 Hours)

  • Refitted slam panel.
  • Cleaned coolant water tank.
  • Cleaned and painted horns.
  • Rewired and cleaned wiring on left hand side of engine bay.

29th May (2 Hours)

  • Cleaned rear of engine bay.
  • Cleaned washer fluid water tank.
  • Cleaned charge cooler reservoir.
  • Cleaned brake fluid holder.
  • Cleaned powersteering rack and bellows.
  • Tried to remove wishbones.

30th May (1.5 Hours)

  • Worked out how to remove wishbones.
  • Started to clean subframe.
  • Fitted powersteering bracket.

4th June (6 Hours)

  • Finished cleaning subframe.
  • Rerouted windscreen washer pump wires.
  • Fitted new radiator fan connectors and resister to radiator fan housing.
  • Cleaned threads on suspension.

5th June (3 Hours)

  • Removed centre section of exhaust.
  • Cleaned half of the threads of all the removed nuts and bolts.
  • Painted gearbox engine mount black.
  • Painted engine mount black.
  • Took manifold back to polishers.

6th June (2 Hours)

  • Fitted gearbox engine mount.
  • Finished cleaning all threads.

11th June (3 Hours)

  • Finished renewing wiring loom.
  • Cleaned thottle body.

12th June (2 Hours)

  • Dismantled centre console and made mould for the radio surround.

13th June (2.5 Hours)

  • Cleaned gearbox.
  • Added fibreglass filler to radio surround mould.

14th June (2 Hours)

  • Finished cleaning gearbox and driveshafts.
  • Added another layer of filler to the radio surround.

18th June (1 Hour)

  • Removed radio surround mould from centre console, trimmed and filled again.

19th June (0.5 Hours)

  • Filled radio surround and filled again.

22nd June

  • Engine rebuilders asked for old pistons and rod so they can work out correct orientation of new ones.

25th June

  • Picked up Chrome exhaust heatshield and lamda probe rail.
  • Took old pistons to engine rebuilders.
  • Engine rebuilders phoned to say there was a major problem with the new rods.

5th July (2 Hours)

  • Masked front engine mount. Sprayed it aluminium using high temperature paint.
  • Decided to also spray the gearbox aluminium so started to strip all attachments to it.
  • Sprayed radio surround with rubber paint but it showed that the filler was not smooth enough. Removed rubber coating and added more filler.

8th July (4 Hours)

  • Cleaned new air intake manifold, masked and painted aluminium.
  • Fitted new front engine mount.
  • Cleaned and painted black old gearbox mount.
  • Cleaned and painted aluminium the drive shaft bracket.
  • Tried to dismantle injector rubber boots but couldn't work out how they came apart.
  • Fitted most of centre console back into place.
  • Painted radio surround but scratched it when fitting.

9th July (2 Hours)

  • Sanded and painted with filler the radio surround.
  • Cleaned throttle and fitted to Plenum chamber.
  • Fitted fuel pressure regulator to fuel rail.
  • CLeaned and painted slave clutch cylinder bracket.

15th July (5 Hours)

  • Fitted front engine mount to engine.
  • Tried to fit reconditioned injectors but noticed that one has a new spray nozzle clip which didn't fit properly or match the others.
  • Cleaned, masked and painted aluminium the upper engine mount.
  • Fitted driveshaft support.
  • Fitted new idler and tensioner pulleys.
  • Fitted oil pressure sender.
  • Removed old exhaust timing wheel off old camshaft.
  • Cleaned powersteering pump and refitted.

17th July (1 Hour)

  • Fitted headgasket and head onto engine block. However I don't know the tightening order.
  • Determined TDC of #1 piston using dial gauge.

18th July (2 Hours)

  • Tried to replace the rubber boots on the ends of the injector wires but couldn't dismantle the plugs.
  • Fitted radio into it's new surround and finished fitting the centre console.
  • Fitted new chip to the diagnostic widget and refitted widget back into dashboard.

20th July (3 Hours)

  • Readjusted timing belt tension.
  • Fitted camshaft cover.
  • Fitted cambelt cover.
  • Fitted injectors to inlet manifold and manifold to engine.

21st July (6 Hours)

  • Fitted spark plugs.
  • Tightened powersteering belt.
  • Fitted water pipe to front of engine.
  • Fitted exhaust manifold.
  • Fitted thermostat.
  • Fitted Lamda probe rail.

22nd July (6.5 Hours)

  • Lifted engine off engine stand and onto ground.
  • Fitted flywheel.
  • Fitted clutch.
  • Fitted gearbox.
  • Fitted rear engine mount to gearbox.
  • Fitted gear selector bracket.
  • Fitted starter motor.

24th July (3 Hours)

  • Tried to fit new wishbones but threads are stripping so fitted old wishbones.
  • Fitted anti-rollbar.
  • Fitted driveshafts but not torqued up.

25th July (2 Hours)

  • Fitted subframe.
  • Fitted powersteering rack to subframe.
  • Bolted rear engine mount to subframe.
  • Removed crane supporting engine.

26th July (2 Hours)

  • Started to fit wiring loom to engine sensors.
  • Started to fit cooland hoses to engine.
  • Fitted coolant expansion tank.
  • Removed charge cooler reservoir.

28th July (5 Hours)

  • First attempt at fitting the new turbo but every orientation seemed to give problems with either the actuator, the actuator bracket or the oil and water feeds.
  • Made brackets for remote oil filter and horns.
  • Discovered the starter motor is missing a lead.

31st July (6 Hours)

  • Another attempt at making a new horn bracket.
  • Trimmed plastic cowel around remote oil filter.
  • Fitted offside wheel arch trim.
  • Fitted coolant and washer water bottles.
  • Found missing starter motor lead and fitted it.
  • Tidied up the wiring loom for the front lights.
  • Second attempt at fitting the new turbo.

1st August (3.5 Hours)

  • Fitted strut brace.
  • Fitted radiator, cooling fans and remaining coolant hoses.
  • Fitted air intake.
  • Plumbed in wastegate actuator.
  • Fitted intercooler.
  • Fitted wheels.
  • Discovered that the turbo outlet is at the wrong angle to attach to the intercooler.

2nd August (6 Hours)

  • Third and final attempt at fitting the new turbo.
  • Fitted downpipe and exhaust.
  • Removed axle stands from under car and lowered to the ground.
  • Wrapped new oil filter pipes in heat wrap.
  • Filled engine and gearbox with oil.

3rd August (0.5 Hours)

  • Fitted battery tray and battery.
  • Filled with coolant.
  • Turned key and engine started.
