Amplifier Installation

In this picture you can see all the cables to be connected to the amplifiers. Coming out of the top of the boot linig are the red positive power cables and the blue audio RCA cables. From the bottom of the boot lining are the white speaker cables, the black negative power cables, the single blue amplifier switch cable and two pairs of multi-strand cables for controlling the amplifiers.

The orginal plan was for the amplifiers to fit against the rear of back seats however the sub woofer amplifier was longer than I had expected and they would not fit. I therefore had to make up a false back for the amplifiers and fit it against the beam that runs along the top of the boot. This provides enough of an angle that the amplifiers can now be attached vertically. I used a peice of high density MDF for this. It took a long time to get the shape perfect such that it would clear the hinge for the boot lid and also fit snugly up against the right hand side. The left hand side has an end peice to close the side and it's all covered in carpet. Holes were made in the MDF for the cables to be fed through from the back. These holes are covered by the covering plates at the ends of the amplifiers.

The board was then screwed to the metal beam at the top of the boot and the cables connected.

This is the final result with the end caps on the amplifiers. It takes up more boost space than I had originally hoped for but the cooling is good and they do look the business when you open the boot.
