Philips Silver Vision Mirrored Bulbs

Part Number12496 SV
 UK : 581

I thought the orange bulbs in the front light clusters spoilt the look of the car means as it has transparent indicator lenses. They would look much better if the bulbs were clear. However funnily enough it's illegal to have clear indicators but Philips sell the next best thing. These are mirrored bulbs such that when unlet they appear to be silver however when lit they glow orange just as bright as a regular orange bulb. Fantastic. The top photo below shows the original bulb and the lower photo shows the mirrored bulb. There is still a hint of orange but it's vasty better.

The bulbs are PY21W style so they are direct replacements for most cars and are 12V 21W with an 'E' mark so they are road legal.
