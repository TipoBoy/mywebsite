Alarm Installation

The standard FIAT alarm fits into a blue socket by the fuse box. This appears to be a convenience socket connected to the standard wiring loom to allow easy installation of an alarm.

The following table is a list of each pin's function. The pin numbers will be correct, however the wire colours may be different as they are from the plug that fitted into the socket and not the colour of the wires going into the socket.

PinWire ColourFunction
1YellowConnect to ground to lock both doors
2Yellow with Black stripeConnect to ground to unlock both doors
3Not Connected 
4Blue with Black stripeConnects to ground when either door is opened
5Blue with Black stripeConnects to ground when either door is opened
6Not Connected 
7PinkPermanent +12V
8Blue with White stripe+12V when ignition switched on
9BlackGround
10Red with Black stripeLeft hand side indicators
11Red with Black stripeRight hand side indicators
12Not Connected
