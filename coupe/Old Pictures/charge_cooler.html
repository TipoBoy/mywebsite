PACE Charge Cooler

This is a general view of the complete installation.

However it's a very tight fit, in fact It's fair to say that it doesn't really fit at all. The charge cooler itself is too high. You can see in the following pictures that I have the indents in the lining and the marks on the top of the charge cooler.

The swirl pot is a pig to fit with the strut brace in place and also causes an indent in the bonnet lining.

The prerad is also tight with my Cadamuro grill as you can see in the picture below. Fortunately it doesn't cause the grill to stick out as it would have looked a mess if I'd have trimmed it.

My pump also leaked when I first fitted it and had to place a jubilee clip around the outlet from the pump to stop it leaking.

What happened to mine after about 18 months was I had a large loss of water and couldn't see where it was coming from. I drove it around for a week topping it up but it eventually got to the point where I couldn't make a journey over 5 miles. I could smell the coolant in the engine bay but could not see a leak. Eventually I took the undertray off and noticed the clip leading to the thermostat was loose. I removed the charge cooler and the fit was so tight it had been rubbing against the clip for long enough to wear compeltely through the metal clip.

The bonnet is also tight to close and open.

It turns out the chargecooler supplied was the wrong one and too tall. PACE replaced it with a brand new shorter cooler for free. This fixed the clearance problems with the underside of the bonnet.
