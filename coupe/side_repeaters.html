Clear Side Repeaters

MHW part number ABCFT-303 13107

These are a direct replacement for the standard orange side repeaters but with a clear lense. The kit is comprehensive including lenses, bulbs, rubber boots, sockets and wiring. The wiring is terminated with plugs to fit directly into the original FIAT wiring. The bulbs are orange but you can hardly notice them. I replaced them as the originals were damaged and I thought that clear would look less visible than orange given the colour of the car.
