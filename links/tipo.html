ITALY

Angelo Orlando's Italian "Sedicivalvole" Web Pages

A page on an Italian web site that's written in Italian but can be translated. It briefly describes a 232 bhp Tipo 16V Racing engine that is used in single seaters and costs a staggering £12,700.

JAPAN

There are two Tipo sites in Japan:

One is run by Tetsu Umeda who owns a Tipo DGT. Tetsu says that in Japan the FIAT Tipo is rare. He wrote his web pages to allow contact between Tipo owners from around the world. He has succeeded with this aim as he is now in contact with over 40 owners, 20 of which are 16V owners. The site is unfortunately in Japanese which makes it difficult to view.

The second Tipo site in Japan is run by Motoyuki Matsusima who owns a Tipo 16V. This again is in Japanese only, however Motoyuki understands English.
